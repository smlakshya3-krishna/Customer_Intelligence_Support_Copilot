
INSTAMART CUSTOMER REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY

1. REFUND ELIGIBILITY
Customers can request a refund if they receive a damaged product, wrong product, or an eligible cancelled order.

2. DAMAGED PRODUCTS
If a customer receives a damaged product, they can request a refund by providing the order details.

3. WRONG PRODUCTS
If a customer receives an incorrect product, they can request a refund or replacement.

4. CANCELLED ORDERS
Customers can request a refund for eligible cancelled orders.

5. REFUND PROCESSING
Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method.

6. PAYMENT ISSUES
If a customer is charged twice for one order, the issue must be reviewed by the payment support team.

7. NON-ELIGIBLE REFUNDS
Refunds are not guaranteed for every request. Each request must be checked according to the order status and refund eligibility.

8. CUSTOMER SUPPORT
Customers can contact the support team for help with refund, payment, delivery, and product-related issues.
